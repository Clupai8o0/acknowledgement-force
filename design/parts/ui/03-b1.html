Amplitude, not decoration

Driven by RMS per buffer converted to dBFS, floored at −58 dB, gamma-shaped, then smoothed with an asymmetric attack and release — 26/s up, 8.5/s down. That asymmetry is most of why a meter reads as voice rather than as noise.

Your syllables travel along it

The line samples a rolling ~2.3 s of history across its width, so the left end is what you are saying now and the right end is where it has got to. A pause shows up as a flat stretch moving across, which is the one thing a level meter cannot show you.

Pinned at both ends

The envelope goes to zero at both edges. A line that runs off the frame is a waveform in a media player; a line that starts and ends inside the panel is a mark someone made.

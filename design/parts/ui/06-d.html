The reviews

The only two surfaces where the app is allowed to go long, because they are the only two you sat down for. D23's rule underneath the whole length table: the app's own prose is short on surfaces it forced you onto and long on surfaces you chose. The gate is forced. A review is chosen. That is the only variable — and it is why illustration is permitted here and banned on the gate.

Why the illustration is allowed here

What repeats must be abstract or absent; what is rare can be vivid. The per-claim engraving regenerates every 28 days — roughly thirteen images a year, each made from a sentence you wrote and something you said out loud.

It inherits the palette

The engraving is black line art on white, knocked out by using its own luminance as the alpha channel and then recoloured to ink3. Not a segmentation model: matting eats thin hatch lines, and cross-hatching is the entire style.

not in the record Which token it recolours to is undecided. accent is disqualified by arithmetic — a full illustration in it would put more of that colour on one screen than the rest of the app uses in a month.

Counts, never a score

19 / 6 / 3 are three numbers side by side. There is no percentage, no grade, no comparison to last window. An AI that scores you becomes an opponent you game or resent — and you can lie to it for free.

The one line the record earns

You came back on the 4th without anyone asking you to is true because of D12: on a first and second miss the app said nothing. The silence earlier is what makes this sentence available now.

Stopping is still free of shame

It sits in the same list, in the same type, as keeping. D4 wants the receipt because a log of what you've abandoned and why is the most useful document about you that could exist — not as punishment.

Why the marks wrap at 14

28 marks at 29 px each is 812 px, and the day numbers are 14 px and do not shrink to fit — so the row has to break. It breaks at 14 rather than wherever the container runs out, which makes each row a fortnight and the two halves of the window directly comparable. D17 forced the break; putting it somewhere meaningful was free.

Force

Every surface of the app, drawn at full size in the Ash palette, with the four locked motions running. This is not the wireframe and not the motion lab — it is the product, screen by screen, at the fidelity you would have to reject or approve before anyone writes Dart.

Rendered 1:1

No screen is scaled. A 17 px sentence on this page is 17 px. If it is hard to read here it will be hard to read at 11pm, which is the only test that matters (D17).

Live, not pictures

Hold the ring in A3 until the circle takes the screen. Click a candidate in A2 to move the accent rule, edit the sentence, pick the mark up in B4 and throw it. The control in the bottom-right replays whatever screen you are looking at.

Cited or tagged

Every screen names the decisions it obeys. Anything not decided is drawn dashed and says so. Nothing undecided is presented as settled.

Dark only

The spikes were dark-only by spec and a light theme is undecided. Showing Ash on a white ground would misrepresent it, so this page stays dark in either OS theme.

Contrast figures above are computed in your browser from the rendered swatch against bg #101112, not copied from the doc. v1 shipped colours nobody had measured and the failures surfaced seven months later.

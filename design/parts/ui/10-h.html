Section H

Not decided

Everything below is open on purpose. None of it is drawn as though it were settled, and none of it should be resolved by someone quietly picking one and building it. Two of these block code: a verdict added after days exist is a migration and a palette change, and a stroke width chosen by accident is how v1 shipped a 1.72:1 mark.

H1

Is there a fourth verdict?

blocks the enumD5 vs D7

D5 says the day settles as kept / partial / broken. D7, the palette note in FORCE-V2 §4 and spikes/SPEC.md all say kept / broken / unsettled. Three places against one, and D7 is the later and more worked-out decision — but partial was never explicitly retracted. Every screen on this page is drawn with three states. A fourth mark needs an eleventh token or a split fill. At 11 px the split reads as a smudge, so the mark grows — and the mark size is what sets the strip's whole geometry.

What it needs from you

My read: partial is a leftover from before D7 existed, and D7's argument actively works against it — the value of the record is that unsettled is not broken, and a fourth state that means "sort of kept" gives every ambiguous night a place to hide. A day that closes as broken, spoken honestly, is worth more than a day that closes as kept; a day that closes as partial is worth almost nothing, because nobody ever has to say what actually happened.

But this is D5's word against D7's and it is your call, not mine. It has to be made before the enum exists: adding a state after days are on disk is a migration and a palette change at the same time.

Three ways out, and they are not equivalent

Carry it with stroke weight — the showcase's own fix, 1.4 px to 1.6 px. Cheapest, keeps D19 untouched, and the middle panel above is what every screen on this page already uses.

Fill it solid — passes comfortably, but then unsettled and broken are both filled discs and the open ring stops carrying "you didn't show up". The shape is doing real work; I would not spend it.

Lighten the hex — that is a change to D19 and goes back to the record. Roughly #5D646A clears 3:1, and it would need re-measuring against bg2 as well, where it currently sits at 2.7:1.

Whichever way it goes: measure it and write the number down. That is the whole difference between this and how v1 shipped.

H3

Fraunces Italic is not in the repo

found while building thisD20 italic + rule

D20 locks the emphasis as italic and a rule. Both font directories — force-old/…/Resources/Fonts/ and spikes/flutter-showcase/assets/fonts/ — contain exactly one Fraunces file, and its axes are opsz, wght, SOFT, WONK. There is no ital axis. Fraunces ships italic as a separate file and nobody has ever added it.

The left has true italic letterforms — a single-storey a, a different f, narrower fitting. The right is the roman leaning over, and on a serif that reads as a rendering fault rather than as emphasis. This is a five-minute fix, but it is only five minutes if someone notices before the Dart is written rather than after the first screenshot looks slightly wrong.

Fix: add Fraunces-Italic[SOFT,WONK,opsz,wght].ttf from google/fonts/ofl/fraunces to the app's font assets and declare it as the italic style of the same family. It is already downloaded into this session's scratchpad, and this page is rendering it.

H4

The landing page — a proposal, not a design

marketing voice undecidedD14 D21 GitHub

COPY.md lists the landing page and marketing register as undecided, with exactly one binding rule: it may not claim a gate you cannot escape. So this is drawn dashed and I am not going to pretend it is settled — it exists so you have something concrete to react to.

force.app

Force keeps the record. You apply the force.

One sentence about who you are. One question a night. Say what actually happened, and the day closes as kept, broken, or unsettled.

You can always close it. That is on purpose — the alternative to a cheap exit is never compliance, it's uninstallation.

Signed and notarised. Not on the App Store — the bypass instructions are in the README, and there is nothing to bypass.

unsettled is not broken. broken means you showed up and lost. unsettled means you didn't show up — and a run of those means the app is dying, which is the thing it most needs to be able to see.

What I deliberately did not do

No screenshots of the gate, no feature grid, no testimonials, no "trusted by", no pricing. A landing page for a product with one screen should be able to be the screen. Whether that is the right instinct is a decision, and it is not in the record.

The claim arrives

The first thing you see, every single morning, for 28 days. It has to land the first time and survive the twenty-eighth.

What I'd watch forWord-by-word is the most affecting on day one and the most likely to feel slow by day twenty. Line-rise is the quickest to read. Ink-bloom is the only one that never asks you to wait for a sequence — which may be the right answer for something you see 28 times.

01b

Emphasis — where the one colour goes

All three use Ink Bloom, so you're comparing the emphasis, not the entrance. The mark lands after the sentence has resolved — the claim reads first, then the app points at the part that can actually be broken.

Why this phrase“I am someone who trains” is unfalsifiable — any day can absorb it. “when I don't feel like it” is the half that can be false, and therefore the only half that can be kept. Marking it puts the one spot of colour in the app on the exact words the whole product is about.

02

Hold to speak

You asked for a big circle that comes up when you hold. Here's that, plus two other ways to show a voice. Amplitude here is synthesised — on device it's your real microphone.

What I'd watch forThe ring is the most beautiful and the most decorative. Bars are the most honest — you can read your own volume off them. Breath is the quietest and the only one that doesn't compete with your own voice for attention.

03

The settle

The day closing. This is the emotional centre of the gate — the one animation that carries meaning rather than polish, because it's the moment the day becomes a fact.

What I'd watch forFling has real spring physics and feels earned. Stamp is decisive and final — closer to a verdict being recorded. Draw is the gentlest, and the only one that would feel the same on a broken day as a kept one, which might be exactly right.

04

On the palettes

All three fix the measured failure in v1's theme: #60646A tested at 3.03:1 and fails the 4.5:1 minimum, and the old outline colour at 1.72:1 made the unsettled ring nearly invisible — hiding the exact state that signals the app is dying.

Ash

Closest to v1. Near-black, bone ink, one oxide red. Kept is bright, broken is a warm ash, unsettled is a hollow ring you can actually see. Quiet, serious, and it lets the engravings carry all the character.

Slate & Brass

Cool blue-black ground with warm brass as the kept colour — so a kept day literally glows and a broken one goes cold blue. The most emotionally legible at a glance, and the furthest from where v1 was.

Plate

Warm dark brown-black with bone ink — the ground an engraving would actually be printed on. The most coherent with the illustration direction you picked, and the only one that doesn't feel like software.

Honest caveatThe display face here is Georgia, not Fraunces — web pages can't load the real font without shipping a 300 KB file, and it fails silently rather than warning you. Judge the motion and the colour from this page; judge the typography on the phone.
